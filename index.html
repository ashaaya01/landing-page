The Status Quo: Cash fraud is hard to see until it is too late.

Today, a stolen or counterfeit note looks like any other. Banks and businesses have no shared way to check a note, report a problem, or learn from what others have found.

No shared record

Each institution works alone, so a note flagged in one place can be accepted in another.

Slow reporting

Theft is reported by phone and on paper, so other banks hear about it too late to act.

Little visibility

Central banks struggle to see how cash moves through the system or where problems start.

One trusted record, connected end to end.

TrackRevoTM links everyone who handles cash to the central bank that issues it.

1

The central bank issues and records

The central bank keeps the official record of issued banknotes and manages the entities allowed to take part.

2

Banks and businesses verify

Tellers, ATMs and counting equipment check notes on the spot and report suspected theft or flagged deposits.

3

Warnings spread fast

Incident and discovery reports are filed and reviewed in one place, so a flagged note is shared with every connected participant within moments.

Built for everyone who touches cash.

Central banks

Manage participants, follow reports as they come in, and understand how cash moves, with each country's data kept fully separate.

Banks & branches

Verify notes at every teller and ATM, manage cash inventory, and handle incident reports with an approval step built in.

Smaller cash businesses

Currency exchange houses and similar businesses get the same protection from a tablet app, with no server to run.

The public

Point a phone at a banknote to check its serial number and report anything suspicious in a few taps.

Security and privacy by design.

Handling money data calls for care. TrackRevoTM is built so each party sees only what it needs.

Customer data stays local

Customer-level cash records remain with each institution. Only anonymised movement data reaches the central bank.

Separate by country

Each central bank operates in its own silo, with no data shared across borders.

Controlled access

Role-based access, reviewed reports and the ability to suspend any participant instantly keep the network trustworthy.
